Privacy

Privacy

This site collects nothing about you itself. Here is what happens when you visit it.

No cookies, analytics or tracking

The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party scripts, fonts or embeds. Its pages and the dashboard load files only from this site.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. As with any web host, each request your browser makes reaches Vercel's servers, which receive technical data such as your IP address, the address requested and your browser's user agent, and Vercel keeps request logs to run and secure its service. How Vercel handles that data is set out in the Vercel privacy policy.

The dashboard runs on your device

The dashboard downloads its program and data, about 6 MB, from this site, then computes the forecast, the regional split and the plan in your browser. The assumptions you change stay on your device: nothing you do in it is sent to us or anyone else, and nothing is uploaded. It saves no settings, so closing the tab discards them; your browser may keep the downloaded files in its normal cache.

Links to other sites

Links to GitHub, the National Power Portal and the other sources lead to sites with their own privacy policies.
