About India Energy 2047

A forecast of the electricity India must generate in FY2047-48, and a regional plan to generate it, built by Team The Losers at the Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru.

What the project is

India Energy 2047 forecasts the electricity India must generate each year up to FY2047-48 (April 2047 to March 2048), with its uncertainty, and recommends a least-cost generation mix for each grid region of the National Power Portal that plays to the region's strengths across coal, solar, wind, natural gas, hydro and nuclear.

The forecast measures energy generated, in TWh. Installed capacity and energy consumption are never inputs to it. It is estimated on the two challenge datasets: generation from the National Power Portal (NPP) and the Renewable India dataset of state population and GSDP. Every other source is secondary and labelled where it is used.

The results page states every number with its range and source, and the dashboard runs the same model in your browser: change an assumption and the forecast, the regional split and the mix recompute.

Who we are

We are Team The Losers, Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru. We built the model, the dashboard and this site as our submission to ExxonMobil INNOTHON 2026.

The competition

In our words, the challenge asks for a data-driven forecast of India's total generation requirement in 2047 and a realistic regional plan for meeting it across energy sources. We registered before registration closed on 9 November 2026, presented at the midpoint review on 18 November and at the final evaluation on 21 November 2026. The grand finale and prize ceremony is on 30 November 2026 in Bengaluru.

This is a team submission, not an official ExxonMobil page.
