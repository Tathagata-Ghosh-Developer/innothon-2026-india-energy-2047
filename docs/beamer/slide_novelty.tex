\textbf{How to present.} Read the middle column as our contribution and the right column as the proof. Each row is a claim the jury can check in the dashboard or the repository.\par\medskip
\textbf{Fair framing of the state of practice.} The CEA plan is a carefully built bottom-up study; its long-range values (2036--37, 2041--42) come from extrapolation with growth rates, and its demand side has a single base path plus a +5\% sensitivity. The IEA explicitly says its scenarios are not forecasts. ExxonMobil's Outlook is a single ``most probable'' projection. None attaches probabilities to electricity demand; that is the gap we fill.\par\medskip
\textbf{Saturation.} Cross-country studies find per-capita electricity flattening with income (Yoo and Lee 2010) and S-shaped appliance adoption (Wolfram, Shelef and Gertler 2012). India's own data show no decline yet, so saturation enters as a sensitivity: an elasticity falling 0.2 per unit of log income gives 4,197\,TWh ($-$11\%).\par\medskip
